Unfolds every lesson at once — for looking back over a term, or for printing. 一次展開所有課程，方便回顧整個學期，或列印。

Opens the phrase cards, the useful-language lists and any held-back answer, and ticks the right option in each question — for going over it together afterwards. 展開所有字卡、可用句型與被隱藏的答案，並標出每題的正確選項，方便之後一起檢討。

Used by the 🔊 buttons. Choosing one plays a sample. 供 🔊 按鈕使用。選擇後會播放一段範例。

Nothing is saved between lessons — this just clears the screen. Your voice and Chinese settings stay. 課程之間不會儲存任何紀錄，這只是清空畫面。你的語音與中文設定會保留。
