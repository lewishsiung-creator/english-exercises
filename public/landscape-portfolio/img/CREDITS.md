Attribution also appears **on the page itself**, under every photograph and in
the lightbox, which is where CC BY actually requires it — a credits file in a
repository is not attribution to somebody reading the portfolio. That comes from
the `credit` field on each image in `../content.js`; remove the photograph and
remove the field together.

## Why these and not better ones

Ando's own drawings and the professional photography of the complex are
copyrighted and were deliberately not used. Commons works here because Japan's
freedom-of-panorama rule (Article 46 of its Copyright Act) allows photographs of
architecture permanently sited in public places to be published freely, so these
are properly licensed rather than merely available.

## Replacing them

If you are building your own portfolio from `content.starter.js`, delete these
four files and this document. Your own photographs need no credit line — drop
the `credit` field and the caption stands alone.

# Drawings

The twelve `.svg` files beside them are drawings made for this study: schematic
plans, sections, diagrams and details, drawn to explain the landscape rather
than traced from anyone's drawings. They carry no third-party licence.

They are plain SVG with their colours written in, because an `<img>` cannot
inherit the page's CSS variables. If you restyle the page, the palette at the
top of each file is what needs changing.
