Meeting Prep會前準備

This page is for one student. Enter the password to open it. 這一頁只給一位學員使用，請輸入密碼開啟。

Settings 設定

Reads your lines. The visitor is given a different voice — a British or Hong Kong one where this device has it. 朗讀你要說的句子。來訪者會自動換成另一種聲音，裝置上有的話，優先用英式或香港英語。

Saves the whole page with the Chinese, every line and every cue card open. In the window that opens, choose "Save as PDF". 把整頁存成 PDF：中文、每一句和每張提示卡都會展開。在跳出的視窗選「另存為 PDF」；iPhone／iPad 請點分享，再選「儲存到檔案」。

This device shows the 自學／教學 switch. The student's plain link never does. 這台裝置會顯示「自學／教學」切換，學生用一般連結看不到。

Ticks are kept on this device only. 勾選只存在這台裝置上。

Forget the password on this device. The page will ask for it next time. 讓這台裝置忘記密碼，下次打開時要重新輸入。
