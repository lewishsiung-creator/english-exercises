中譯和字義都收在「中」後面，可以先問學生再點開。規則和筆記不收——那是你要講的話，不是學生要答的題。教學模式不記錄任何東西。 Meanings and translations wait behind the 中 chips; the rules and notes do not, because they are what you say rather than what they answer. Teaching mode records nothing.
