怎麼用 · How to use

和孩子一起一頁一頁看。按「🔊 Listen」或點任何一句英文，就會念出來（不會自動播放）；中文只顯示文字，方便大人一起看懂。每頁下方的「💬 與孩子討論」是給大人問孩子的問題。

Go through it page by page with your child. Tap “Listen” or any English line to hear it; nothing plays by itself. The Chinese is there to read, not spoken. “💬 Talk about it” has a question for you to ask.

聲音 · Sound

想要更自然的聲音：iPhone／iPad 到「設定 → 輔助使用 → 朗讀內容 → 聲音 → 英文」，下載 Ava（高品質或增強音質），再重新打開這頁，就會自動使用。
For a more natural voice on iPhone/iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, download Ava (Premium or Enhanced), then reopen this page.

    參觀資訊 · Visitor info

    • 出發前：記得讓孩子帶健保卡。
      Before you go: pack your child’s NHI card.
    • 開放：週二至週日 09:00–17:00（16:30 停止入館），週一休館。11/13（五）是一般開放日。
      Open Tue–Sun 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30); closed Mondays. Fri 13 Nov is a normal open day.
    • 兒童廳：平日場次 09:30–12:00、14:00–16:30，現場排隊，每場限 100 人（含大人）。學校團體是否安排，請以老師通知為準。
      Children’s Hall, weekdays 9:30–12:00 and 14:00–16:30, queue on site, 100 people per session. Whether the class goes is the school’s plan.
    • 參觀須知：展場內不飲食（可在飲水機喝水）、不奔跑追逐、不大聲喧嘩；沒有標示可以摸的展品不觸碰、不靠玻璃；不開閃光燈、不用自拍棒。六歲以下需大人陪同。
      No food inside (water fountains are fine), no running or shouting, touch only where a sign says so, no flash or selfie sticks. Under-sixes must be with an adult.
    • 地址：臺南市安南區長和路一段 250 號 · 06-356-8889

    Facts from the museum’s official website, 12 Nov 2026. The “Story of Taiwan” pages are a simplified retelling for six-year-olds.
