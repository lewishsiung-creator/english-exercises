Settings 設定

Used by the speaker buttons. Choosing one plays a sample. 供喇叭按鈕使用。選擇後會播放一段範例。

Unfolds the whole notebook — for looking back over several lessons at once, or for printing. 展開整本筆記，方便一次回顧多堂課，或列印。

Fills in the gaps, matches every pair and opens the phrase cards and corrections — for going over an exercise together. 填入所有空格、配對所有片語，並展開字卡與訂正，方便一起檢討練習。

Teaching mode records nothing. The 記住了 marks a student made at home stay on his own device and are not shown here. 教學模式不記錄任何東西。學生在家標記的「記住了」留在他自己的裝置上，這裡不會顯示。

No answer is saved — this clears the screen for a fresh run. Your voice and speed stay. 作答不會儲存，這只是清空畫面、重新練一次。語音與速度設定會保留。

This device shows the 自學／教學 switch. A student opening the plain link never sees it. 這台裝置會顯示「自學／教學」切換。學生用一般連結打開時看不到。
